WHAT'S A CONEY DOG?

If you didn't grow up near Detroit, chances are nobody's ever handed you a real Coney dog. Down here in Florida, we're fixing that.

A true Detroit Coney is simple, and it's perfect: a hot dog, topped with beefy chili (no beans), a stripe of bright yellow mustard, and a scatter of diced onions. That's it. No fuss, no gimmicks — just the combo Detroit's been perfecting for over a century.

  • Hot dog
  • Beef chili (no beans)
  • Yellow mustard
  • Diced onions
